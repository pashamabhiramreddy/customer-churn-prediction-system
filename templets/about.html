About ChurnPredict

What We Do

ChurnPredict is an advanced machine learning application that analyzes customer data to predict the likelihood of customer churn. Using a Random Forest classifier trained on real-world customer data, we provide accurate predictions to help businesses retain their valued customers.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower businesses with data-driven insights to understand customer behavior and make proactive decisions. By identifying at-risk customers early, companies can implement targeted retention strategies and improve customer lifetime value.

Why Churn Prediction Matters

Customer churn directly impacts revenue and growth. It costs 5-25x more to acquire a new customer than to retain an existing one. Our system helps you identify and retain high-value customers before they leave.
